ReachMap wireframes

Stage-4 layout skeletons and interaction storyboards for the Chennai transit reachability map. Behavior spec: docs/ux.md (wins on conflict). Visual target: the companion hi-fi mockup artifact.

One surface, two cards. The map is full-viewport; the only chrome is the brand chip (top-left) and the dial (bottom-left). No header bar, no sidebar, no legend box — the ruler inside the dial is the legend.

Bands render as rings (set difference), light→dark outward, so fills never stack and each ring keeps its own hue. Stroke on every contour.

Attribution is the MapLibre control plus the ODbL feed credit; the dial never overlaps it.

Flow F1–F2 · first load, then move the origin

F1 · t=0
dial: defaults
Tue · 08:30
hint: "Tap anywhere…"

Default isochrone (Chennai Central, Tue 08:30) is already painted — a static poster first, the live map crossfades in. Zero interaction required.

F2 · click
pin moves instantly
bands fade to 35%
worker computes <150 ms

Old bands dim while computing; no spinner unless the engine is still loading (then a determinate ring around the pin).

F2 · done
readout updates:
coords · ≈N stops in 60 min

New bands crossfade in (~200 ms). Readout row is aria-live. A transit-desert click yields the walk-only disc + honest one-liner, never an error.

States

StateTreatment
Engine loadingSlim progress line in the dial ("Loading timetable · 4.9 MB", real %). Map pans/zooms freely; clicks queue — pin drops now, bands arrive when ready (determinate ring around pin).
ComputingBands at 35% opacity + pin pulse. prefers-reduced-motion: opacity step only, no pulse.
Transit desertWalk-only disc; readout: "Walk-only from here — no transit reachable within the hour." Not an error.
Out of coveragePin stays exactly where tapped; bands cleared; readout: "Outside the covered area for this feed."
Basemap outageStyle falls back (PMTiles extract → graticule). Bands unaffected. Quiet toast.
Data note (ⓘ)Feed name/version, ODbL credit, known gaps, walking-model constants. Honesty as a feature.

Ruler interaction

Hovering (desktop) or tapping (touch) a ruler segment dims the other three rings to 15% — the instant answer to "which ring is 30 minutes?". Tapping again clears. Rings themselves are never click targets; the whole map surface belongs to origin selection.
